PROMOTE-BIO: Advancing Personalized Shoulder Medicine through Automated Biomechanical Modeling: A Proof-of-Concept in Glenohumeral Osteoarthritis
https://promote-bio.tecnico.ulisboa.pt

PROMOTE-BIO is a multidisciplinary research initiative at the intersection of computational biomechanics, medical imaging, and data-driven modeling. Our goal is to transform musculoskeletal healthcare by streamlining the creation of personalized shoulder models.
