### Advancing Personalized Shoulder Medicine through Automated Biomechanical Modeling: A Proof-of-Concept in Glenohumeral Osteoarthritis (PROMOTE-BIO)

**PROMOTE-BIO** is a multidisciplinary research initiative focused on transforming shoulder healthcare through personalized computational biomechanics. By integrating medical imaging, musculoskeletal modeling, and finite element analysis, we aim to streamline the creation of subject-specific models to support earlier diagnosis and personalized treatment for **Glenohumeral Osteoarthritis (OA)**.

---

## 🎯 Main Objectives

* **Automate Model Generation:** Streamlining the transition from raw medical imaging (MRI/CT) to high-fidelity biomechanical models.
* **Integrated Analysis:** Combining musculoskeletal and finite element modeling for a comprehensive view of joint mechanics.
* **Clinically-Oriented Tools:** Developing user-friendly software designed for both research and clinical application.
* **Reliability & Validation:** Benchmarking automated workflows against traditional modeling to ensure scientific accuracy.
* **Diagnostic Innovation:** Exploring the potential of biomechanical markers to identify early-stage OA-related changes.
* **Clinical Translation:** Bridging the gap between advanced computational research and real-world clinical practice.

---

## 📅 Project Status & Open Science

PROMOTE-BIO is committed to the principles of **Open Science and Reproducibility**. 

To ensure the integrity and clinical reliability of our tools, the project follows a **staged release plan**:

1. **Current Phase:** We are currently in the **Development and Technical Implementation** phase. Our efforts are focused on building the core computational workflows and establishing the integration between imaging and modeling modules.
2. **Future Release:** Once the framework has reached technical maturity and has undergone scientific validation, the software tools and comprehensive documentation will be made publicly available here.
